NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc is bundled here as a test fixture for CJK (Japanese/
Chinese/Korean) font handling tests: font-family fallback selection
(core/src/fonts/collection.rs), shaping of non-Latin text (core/src/fonts),
and PDF font-embedding/subsetting tests (core/src/pdf). It is distributed
under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, a permissive license that
explicitly allows embedding, bundling, and redistribution.
